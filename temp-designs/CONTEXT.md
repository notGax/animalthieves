# Animal Thieves

Language for the characters, transformations, and raid spaces in the cooperative heist game.

## Player Forms

**Slime**:
The default, entirely limbless form of every player character and the shared visual identity players return to between transformations. It has a low, spreading cartoon slime silhouette, a bright player-customizable body color, and exactly two tiny dark bead eyes rather than humanoid anatomy or realistic creature detail.
_Avoid_: Animal, base animal

**Animal Form**:
A temporary bipedal species form adopted by a **Slime**, retaining recognizable features of that player while granting species-specific abilities. Every form has exactly two tiny dark bead eyes, matching the **Slime**, as a shared visual trace of the player's identity.
_Avoid_: Class, permanent character, animal player

**Animal Character**:
Any animal-based character, including **Animal Forms**, guards, and bosses. Animal characters stand on two legs and have forelimbs capable of handling equipment; the **Slime** is the only limbless character type.
_Avoid_: Quadruped, realistic animal

**Transformation Item**:
A rare boss reward that unlocks access to its associated **Animal Form**.
_Avoid_: Skin, character unlock

**Rat Form**:
The rat species **Animal Form**, defined visually by a lanky, narrow, hunched bipedal body, long thin limbs, oversized hands and feet, a pointed muzzle, large uneven ears, a long angular tail, tiny bead eyes, and a compact understated mouth. Its overall standing height is approximately equal to the **Fish Form**.
_Avoid_: Mouse form, squat rat

**Cat Form**:
The cat species **Animal Form**, defined visually by a tall, narrow prowler body, long limbs, oversized hands and feet, a forward hunch, angular ears and muzzle, tiny bead eyes, and a long tail held in an exaggerated question-mark curve.
_Avoid_: Compact cat, quadruped cat

**Fish Form**:
The fish species **Animal Form**, based on a stylized mackerel with a skinny upright body, a hunched angular silhouette, tiny bead eyes, naturally sized pectoral fins used as hands, and an intact forked caudal fin used as its entire standing base.
_Avoid_: Guppy, large fish, deep-sea fish, mermaid, human-legged fish, separate tail-legs

## Raid Spaces

**Raid Building**:
An enclosed business location that players enter to steal merchandise and eventually confront its boss.
_Avoid_: Dungeon, open-world area

**Room**:
A visually distinct interior space within a **Raid Building**, sized for navigation, physical loot, environmental interactions, and encounters.
_Avoid_: Level, entire building

## Example Dialogue

**Artist:** “Am I designing the rat as a separate player character?”
**Designer:** “No. Design it as an **Animal Form** of the **Slime**, so the transformed player remains recognizable.”
